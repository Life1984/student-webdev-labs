Chrome Dino Game

The Chrome Dino Game was released in 2014 by Google. It appears when users lose internet connection in Chrome.

Players control a dinosaur that jumps over obstacles and avoids enemies. It is simple, fun, and addictive.

Untitled Goose Game

Untitled Goose Game was released in 2019 by House House. It is a puzzle-stealth game where players control a mischievous goose.

Players complete tasks by sneaking around, stealing objects, and annoying villagers in creative ways.

Flappy Bird

Flappy Bird was released in 2013 by Dong Nguyen. Players guide a bird through gaps between green pipes.

The game became famous for its simple controls, high difficulty, and addictive score-chasing gameplay.
